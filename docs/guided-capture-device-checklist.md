# Guided capture device and accessibility checklist

Automated journey coverage validates setup → excluded practice → recording →
review/recovery → report with simulated standards-based camera APIs. It does
not substitute for the physical-device gate below.

## Required scenarios

- Grant, deny and recover camera permission.
- Switch front/rear cameras after labels become available.
- Confirm negotiated container/codec is playable and uploadable.
- Complete readiness and verify only one stable instruction is announced.
- Complete a practice rep and confirm it cannot enter the reported count.
- Record, stop, review, discard and record again.
- Background the browser and disconnect/lock the camera during recording;
  confirm captured footage is preserved and clearly labelled interrupted.
- Exit actively recording; test both Save and review and Discard recording.
- Use the recovered recording through the evidence report.

## Accessibility

- Complete every action with keyboard and switch-control equivalents.
- Verify visible focus, 200% zoom, landscape/portrait reflow and reduced motion.
- With VoiceOver/TalkBack, confirm permission errors, countdown, Recording,
  interruption, readiness prompt and report selection are announced once.
- Ensure controls stay outside the athlete framing area and meet 44px targets.
- Confirm camera guidance is not colour-only and audio cues are optional.
